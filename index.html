/ About

I am a PhD scientist in the Biological Systems and Engineering Division at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. Over 11 years I have worked across the biofuels pipeline: plant cell-wall biology, then biomass pretreatment with ionic liquids and amines, and along the way I built the Feedstocks-to-Fuels and Products (F2F) pipeline from scratch, taking samples from deconstruction through fermentation to analysis. In the lab, I am the person colleagues come to when an experiment is stuck.

Now I am adding the second half: AI. I build LLM agents and fine-tune models, and what I care about most is honest evaluation, knowing when an AI system is actually working and when it is only confident. My UC Berkeley Professional Certificate in Machine Learning and AI starts in November 2026. My aim is the intersection: AI aimed at real biology, judged by someone who has run the experiments.

Gemma 4 Developer Agent (Kaggle competition)

Built and evaluated an AI agent that fixes bugs in real Python codebases. Fine-tuned the 31B model with a LoRA adapter trained on 354 worked examples, then ran a controlled A/B test against the base agent on the same tasks. The evidence said the adapter was not the lever, so the simpler base agent stayed as a final candidate.
